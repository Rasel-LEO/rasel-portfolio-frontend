## Profile

Agricultural engineer and researcher building intelligent farm systems — from embedded
hardware, robotics and IoT to AI models, data analytics and software. Research on
IoT- and AI-edge-based automation for poultry, aquaculture, irrigation and farm machinery;
author of peer-reviewed papers in _Smart Agricultural Technology_, _Discover Applied
Sciences_ and ASABE proceedings. Also develops web, app and embedded software.
